Doug Corbitt for State Representative

Meet Doug.

I'm Doug Corbitt, and I'm running for the Arkansas House of Representatives from District 54.

I grew up in the Congo (DRC) where my physician parents ran a 200-bed hospital in a remote village called "Kapanga." From them I learned that every individual is precious, that every person should be treated with dignity, and that everyone deserves our very best.

Those values ultimately led me to a career in education, and for the last twenty-five years I have taught in the Honors College at the University of Central Arkansas. One of my favorite courses there has been a seminar on democracy as a moral ideal, not just a way of selecting our leaders.

A moral ideal is a goal worthy of our best efforts, and when we understand what "democracy" really means, we understand why it is worth fighting for. When we encourage everyone's full participation in the processes, practices, and institutions that govern our lives, we build a better world for all. When we help make possible everyone's full participation, we build a better world for all. When we listen to everyone's input, we discover better solutions and thereby build a better world for all. And when we view each other as fellow citizens - rather than as opponents to be crushed, humiliated, and defeated - we build a better world for all.

Why I'm running.

I'm running because I have fallen in love with Arkansas - my home for the past thirty-eight years. I'm running because I believe in the people of this amazing state and in what we can do together. I'm running because I believe that, for all the good things the state government has done and is doing, there is so much more we could do to stimulate economic growth, improve health outcomes, support our public schools, strengthen our communities, and help struggling families make ends meet.

Practical priorities

Opportunity for every Arkansan.

Lowering costs for families

Affordable childcare, healthcare access, first-time home mortgages, and targeted relief for farmers.

Stimulating economic growth

Low-interest, short-term loans for people starting or expanding small businesses.

Promoting safe communities

More support for mental health and drug treatment programs.

Supporting public education

Means-tested vouchers, fair pay for experienced teachers, and consistent teacher accreditation standards.

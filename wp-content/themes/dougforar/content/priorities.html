Doug believes state government should focus on the essentials that help Arkansas families and communities thrive.

Lowering costs for families

  • By investing in affordable childcare
  • By expanding access to healthcare
  • By making it easier for first-time home buyers to get mortgages
  • By offering farmers state tax rebates to offset the high costs of diesel fuel and fertilizer

Stimulating economic growth

  • By making low-interest, short-term loans available to people seeking to start or expand small businesses

Promoting safe communities

  • By increasing spending on mental health and drug treatment programs

Supporting public education

  • By requiring means testing for educational vouchers
  • By paying experienced teachers salaries commensurate with their years of service
  • By requiring private schools receiving educational vouchers to meet the same teacher accreditation standards that public schools must meet

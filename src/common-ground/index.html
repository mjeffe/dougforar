Meet Doug

District 54 has been home for 36 years.

Doug and his wife, Peg, raised their daughters here and built their life here. After more than 30 years as an educator at a public institution, Doug understands how access to opportunity can change a life.

He is running to listen, respond, and serve every constituent, regardless of party.

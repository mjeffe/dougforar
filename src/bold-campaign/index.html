Meet Doug Corbitt

A record of service. A commitment to listen.

Doug has lived in District 54 for 36 years. He and his wife, Peg, raised their daughters here, and he has spent more than 30 years educating Arkansas students at a public institution.

Now he is ready to bring that experience to the State House and represent every neighbor, regardless of party.

A life in District 54

Husband. Father. Educator. Neighbor.

Doug and his wife, Peg, have called District 54 home for 36 years. They raised their daughters here and built their life here.

After more than 30 years as an educator at a public institution, Doug understands that opportunity grows when communities invest in people. He is running to serve every constituent, regardless of party.

Practical priorities

Opportunity for every Arkansan.

Focus state government on the essentials that help families and communities thrive.

Strong Public Schools

Keep public dollars working for Arkansas students, educators, and neighborhood schools.

Healthcare Close to Home

Protect access to quality, affordable care for rural families and expectant mothers.

Open, Responsive Government

Protect transparency, listen to constituents, and account for every tax dollar.

Doug Corbitt for State Representative

Meet Doug.

Husband. Father. Educator. Neighbor. Doug and his wife, Peg, have called District 54 home for 36 years. They raised their daughters here and built their life here.

Why I'm running.

District 54 deserves a representative who listens, responds, and works for everyone. After more than 30 years as an educator at a public institution, Doug understands that opportunity grows when communities invest in people.

He is running to serve every constituent, regardless of party, and to help make government work for us, not against us.
